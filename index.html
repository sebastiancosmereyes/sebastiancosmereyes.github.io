About

Curious about how flight works.

My interest in aerospace began with a fascination for flight and difficult physics problems. Today, I apply fluid mechanics to problems in aerodynamic design and propulsion.

I am especially drawn to work that turns an idea into evidence: a model, a fabricated test article, a measurement, and a result that can be explained clearly.
