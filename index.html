About Me

I am an aerospace engineering student with a passion for propulsion, aerodynamic design, and the physics of flight. My work combines fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, gas dynamics, experimental research, and hands-on fabrication. I am especially interested in engineering problems that connect analytical models with real hardware and test data.

Featured Projects

Selected work in aerodynamic analysis, experimental fluid mechanics, propulsion, and aerospace research.

Bell X-1 Aerodynamic Analysis

MATLAB-based subsonic and supersonic analysis of the Bell X-1 across Mach 0–1.45, including panel methods, compressibility corrections, pressure distributions, and wave-drag estimation.

MATLAB Aerodynamics Supersonic Flow

Rotating Cylinder Water-Tunnel Research

Designed and fabricated a variable-speed rotating-cylinder apparatus for water-tunnel testing. Used particle image velocimetry to study circulation, vorticity, lift, drag, and wake behavior.

PIV Experimental Fluids Arduino

Martian Dust Mitigation Research

Researching rotating-cylinder flow as a potential method to mitigate dust accumulation on Martian solar panels and developing an associated PIV experiment.

Research Mars Fluid Mechanics

Bipropellant Rocket Engine

Led a four-person team through the design, analysis, manufacturing, and test firing of a 20-lbf bipropellant rocket engine, including pressure-loss analysis and injector, chamber, and nozzle sizing.
