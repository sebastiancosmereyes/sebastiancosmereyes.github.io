About

What I'm building toward

My interest in aerospace started early, through my dad — a mechanical engineer who showed me how things worked. One memory has stuck with me: watching Space Shuttle Endeavour ride into Los Angeles on its carrier aircraft, low over the city. I was hooked on flight after that, and every takeoff on trips home to Puerto Rico has reinforced it since.

Today that curiosity has allowed me to dive into the world of aeorodynamics and fluid mechanics; reverse-engineering historical aircraft geometry, building a water tunnel test apparaturs, running PIV experiments to see flow physics I'd otherwise only be able to calculate, and much, much more! I'm drawn to problems that sit between analysis and hardware — where a model has to hold up against something you can actually measure.

I'm currently pursuing my B.S. and M.S. in Aerospace Engineering at Cal Poly SLO, with a minor in Physics. On the side, I work as a Shop Technician supporting personal, club, and innovative manufacturing projects for the college. I am also involved in a Water Sports Design Club as the Hydrodynamics Lead; all while being a Thermo-Fluid Dynamics teacher's assistant.
