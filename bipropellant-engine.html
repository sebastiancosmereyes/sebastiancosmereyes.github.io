Bipropellant Rocket Engine

Team design, analysis, manufacturing, and test firing of a 20-lbf bipropellant rocket engine with SLO Propulsion Technologies.

Role
Team lead (4-person team)
Thrust class
20 lbf
Organization
SLO Propulsion Technologies
Program
Cal Poly SLO

Objective

Lead a four-person team through the full lifecycle of a small bipropellant rocket engine — from design and analysis through manufacturing and test firing — at a 20-lbf thrust class.

Context & scope

This project was completed with SLO Propulsion Technologies. Because it involved a team, shared hardware, and information that isn't solely mine to publish, this page intentionally stays high-level. It describes the type of work and my role in it rather than specific propellant, performance, or test data.

My role & contributions

I led the four-person team. My individual technical contributions included calculating system pressure losses and sizing the injector orifices, combustion chamber, and nozzle. The design, manufacturing, and test firing were team efforts coordinated across all four members.

Engineering approach & tools

  • System-level pressure-loss calculations
  • Injector orifice sizing
  • Combustion chamber sizing
  • Nozzle sizing
  • Team coordination across design, manufacturing, and test
